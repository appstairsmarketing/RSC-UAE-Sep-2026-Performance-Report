📊 Executive Summary
Account Overall — Better Quality, Higher Cost
September was a better month for lead quality than for cost. Booking rates improved on every platform, and Instagram DMs remain our main engine. But we spent more, and Snapchat and Google got slightly more expensive per result. Telesales feedback is still incomplete, so true quality is only partly visible.
📸 Instagram Strong
Our best platform this month. Bringing Eyelid ads back added the cheapest messages and nearly doubled volume, and more of the logged DMs turned into bookings. LASIK messages are getting more expensive, so we need new creatives and doctor videos for LASIK. Lead-form ads are effectively off.
👻 Snapchat Good — follow-up too slow
Lead quality is improving, and one ad (the Dr Tamer reel) delivers most of the leads at a good price. The weak points are a slowly rising cost per lead and half of leads still not being reached.
🔍 Google Ads Mixed
Cheaper clicks, more visibility and double the appointment bookings, but fewer conversions overall and a cost per conversion that is still high. Brand keywords do the work.

💡 Recommendations for October
📞
More accurate, up-to-date telesales feedback
Feedback is still partial — only 70 of 252 Instagram messages have a logged status, and "No Answer" is the biggest outcome on both Instagram (26 of 70) and Snapchat (26 of 53). We recommend telesales log every contact promptly, so next month reflects real lead quality rather than follow-up gaps.
🎥
More doctor videos & patient success stories
Doctor-led and patient-story content continues to perform best — the Dr Tamer reel is again the top Snapchat ad. We recommend more short doctor explainers, real patient stories and before/after cases, and building on the new Dr Mohamed Nagy videos, which also match what people search for organically.
💝
New LASIK creatives & keep Eyelid running
LASIK messages cost more this month (45.99 AED vs 38.52 in August), while Eyelid is our cheapest DM source (27.39 AED/msg). We recommend new LASIK creatives and doctor videos to bring the cost down, and keeping Eyelid running.
👨‍⚕️
Videos with the full doctor team
We recommend short videos with the other doctors, so the full team of the center is shown. Each video should focus on the treatment itself and show how painless and easy it is, to build trust and ease patients' fear before they book.
✂️
Reuse Dr Mohamed Nagy footage to lower the cost per message
The cost per message on Dr Mohamed Nagy's videos was too high. We recommend AI reels built from his best existing shots, focused on the treatment and how painless and easy it is, with strong hooks and clear calls to action.
